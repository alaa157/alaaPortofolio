About me

Quality isn't a phase. It's how I build.

I'm Alaa Adel, a freelance Software QA and Test Engineer with over five years of experience helping web and mobile teams catch defects before their customers do. I started as a manual tester documenting edge cases line by line, and grew into someone who designs full test strategies — from requirement analysis to automated regression suites running in CI/CD.

I've worked with early-stage startups shipping their first MVP and with established teams maintaining products used by thousands of people daily. My approach is simple: understand the product like a user, test it like an attacker, and report bugs like an engineer — clear, reproducible, and prioritized.

When I'm not hunting bugs, I'm refining test automation frameworks in Selenium and Playwright, or writing internal documentation so a team's QA process doesn't live only in my head.

How I work

A predictable process, every engagement

The same five-stage workflow whether it's a two-day audit or a three-month partnership.

01

Requirement Analysis

Reviewing specs, user stories and designs to understand what "working correctly" actually means.

02

Test Planning

Mapping test cases, priority areas and the right mix of manual vs. automated coverage.

03

Execution

Running manual and automated tests across environments, devices and browsers.

04

Reporting

Logging clear, reproducible bug reports with severity, priority and supporting evidence.

05

Retesting

Verifying fixes, running regression, and signing off before release.
