How I Work

My Process

A structured, transparent approach that keeps you informed and your product on track.

01 Requirement Analysis

I start by deeply understanding your product — its users, core flows, acceptance criteria, and business goals. I review documentation, meet with stakeholders, and identify critical paths and potential risk areas.

02 Test Planning

Based on the analysis, I create a detailed test plan covering scope, strategy, entry/exit criteria, test data requirements, environments, and timeline. You approve the plan before any testing begins.

03 Test Execution

I execute test cases systematically, document results in real-time, and report defects with clear reproduction steps. For automated projects, I build and run test suites in parallel with manual testing efforts.

04 Reporting

You receive comprehensive reports with test coverage metrics, defect summaries, risk assessments, and quality recommendations. I provide daily standup updates and a final test summary report.

05 Retesting & Closure

After fixes are deployed, I perform thorough retesting and regression testing to ensure nothing broke. I provide a final sign-off with a quality gate assessment and release readiness recommendation.
