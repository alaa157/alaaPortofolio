How I work

A QA process designed to reduce surprises.

Clear checkpoints keep testing measurable, collaborative, and aligned with product risk.

01

Requirement Analysis

Clarify expected behavior, business rules, acceptance criteria, and risk areas.

02

Test Planning

Define scope, test scenarios, environments, data, priorities, and exit criteria.

03

Execution

Run manual and automated tests, explore edge cases, and capture evidence.

04

Reporting

Communicate defects with clear reproduction steps, impact, severity, and context.

05

Retesting

Verify fixes, run targeted regression, and confirm release readiness.
